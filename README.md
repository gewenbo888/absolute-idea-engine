# Absolute Idea Engine · 绝对理念引擎

**The Search for the Ultimate Principle Behind Reality, Mind, and Civilization.**
From matter to mind. From thought to reality. From fragmented knowledge to unified understanding.

A massive, bilingual (EN / 中文) single-page exploration of humanity's search for the ultimate organizing principle of reality — across 18 parts (the Absolute Idea, the history of ultimate explanations, matter, information, life, consciousness, intelligence, language, science & unity, emergence, the Hegelian dialectic, civilization, technology, AI, the Unified Knowledge Graph, the cosmic perspective, future civilization, and the open question), each with its own interactive visualization, plus a curated "AI Philosopher" guide. It never claims certainty — it invites exploration.
